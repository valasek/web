Looking for something good to read? Here's my list of books, essays and presentations that have inspired me most over the years – at work and in life. Pick whatever catches your eye and dive in.

## At work

### Blogs and presentations

- **[Netflix Culture Memo](https://jobs.netflix.com/netflix-culture.pdf)** – how Netflix builds a culture where people do the best work of their lives
  - and the original version: **[Freedom & Responsibility Culture](https://www.scribd.com/document/127499280/Netflix-s-Famous-Culture-Guide)**

### Books

- **[Radical Candor](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Radical+Candor+Kim+Scott&i=stripbooks)** – Be a Kick-Ass Boss Without Losing Your Humanity · *Kim Scott*
- **[Start with Why](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Start+with+Why+Simon+Sinek&i=stripbooks)** – How Great Leaders Inspire Everyone to Take Action · *Simon Sinek*
- **[Incorruptible](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Incorruptible+Eric+Ries&i=stripbooks)** – Why Good Companies Go Bad and How Great Companies Stay Great · *Eric Ries*
- **[The Lean Startup](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Lean+Startup+Eric+Ries&i=stripbooks)** – How Constant Innovation Creates Radically Successful Businesses · *Eric Ries*
- **[Factfulness](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Factfulness+Hans+Rosling&i=stripbooks)** – Ten Reasons We're Wrong About the World – and Why Things Are Better Than You Think · *Hans Rosling, Ola Rosling, Anna Rosling Rönnlund*
- **[Inspired the Baťa Way](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Inspired+the+Bata+Way+Koncitikova&i=stripbooks)** – How to Be Stronger and Happier by Adopting Thomas Baťa's Principles to Live By · *Gabriela Končitíková*

## For personal growth

### Books

- **[The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+7+Habits+of+Highly+Effective+People+Covey&i=stripbooks)** – Powerful Lessons in Personal Change · *Stephen R. Covey*
- **[Atomic Habits](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Atomic+Habits+James+Clear&i=stripbooks)** – Tiny Changes, Remarkable Results · *James Clear*
- **[Thinking, Fast and Slow](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Thinking+Fast+and+Slow+Kahneman&i=stripbooks)** · *Daniel Kahneman*
- **[Sapiens](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Sapiens+Yuval+Noah+Harari&i=stripbooks)** – A Brief History of Humankind · *Yuval Noah Harari*
- **[Nature and the Human Soul](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Nature+and+the+Human+Soul+Bill+Plotkin&i=stripbooks)** – Cultivating Wholeness and Community in a Fragmented World · *Bill Plotkin*
