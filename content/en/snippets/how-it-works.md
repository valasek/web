### How it works

1. **Book a free 20-minute intro call** on [findacoach.eu](https://findacoach.eu/coach/valasek). No preparation needed - just think about what you want to change and what a good outcome would look like.
2. **We agree on goals and format.** Together we choose coaching or mentoring, a single session or a package, and online or in-person meetings.
3. **Regular sessions.** Usually 60 minutes, **online, by phone, or in person** in Bratislava (e.g. [HubHub](https://www.hubhub.com/location/bratislava-nivy-tower/)), in Prague, or at a place we agree on. Between sessions you can reach me by phone or email.

*A session can be cancelled no later than 12 hours in advance; otherwise it counts as completed.*
