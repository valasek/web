#### Frequently asked questions

**Is it confidential?**

Yes. Everything we discuss stays between us, in line with the ICF Code of Ethics. If your company pays, I only confirm attendance – never the content.

**Can my employer pay for it?**

Yes. I invoice the company directly at the company rate. Coaching and mentoring are often covered by the education or development budget – ask your manager or HR.

**How many sessions will I need?**

A specific topic or situation can often be handled in 1–3 sessions. A lasting change in how you lead or live usually takes 3–6 months – that's why the Focus and Growth packages exist.

**Online or in person?**

Both work well. Most clients meet online; in person we can meet in Bratislava, Prague or at your company.

**Which language?**

English, Slovak or Czech.
