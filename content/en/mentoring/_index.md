### Mentoring for IT managers and tech leads

Learn to lead people, delegate and handle difficult situations from someone who has been leading IT teams for 15 years – and still does.

[**Book a free 20-minute intro call →**](https://findacoach.eu/coach/valasek)

![](/images/mentoring.jpg)

#### Is this for you?

Clients most often come with:

- **a first management role** – from great engineer to leader of people,
- **delegation and workload** – too much on your plate and your team is waiting for you,
- **feedback** – giving it, receiving it, and the conversations you keep postponing,
- **team motivation and leadership** – keeping people engaged and performing,
- **turnover and backups** – what happens when a key person leaves,
- **corporate environment** – politics, stakeholders and getting things done in a big organization,
- **clients and projects** – communication, expectations and difficult situations,
- **prioritization and time management**,
- **confidence and career planning** – your next step as a leader.

#### What changes

- **You know what to do** in situations that used to paralyse you.
- **You delegate** and get time back for what only you can do.
- **Your team trusts you** because you communicate clearly and fairly.

#### Why me

- **Many years in the field.** 20 years in IT, 15 of them in management, including leading 42 people in an international company. Today I lead a delivery team at Siemens Healthineers.
- **I see what's behind the scenes.** I notice and name the things that deeply affect a company, team or individual, but nobody talks about.
- **Essential questions.** As an ICF-certified coach I don't just hand out advice – I ask questions that bring new perspectives.
- **Results.** I'm structured and care about real value for you.
